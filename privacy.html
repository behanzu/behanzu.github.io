Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how YT Automation ("we", "us", or "our") handles and protects information when you connect and authorize your YouTube account with our automation management dashboard.

1. YouTube API Services

Our application uses the YouTube API Services to manage video scheduling, uploads, and channel analytics. By using this application, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Access and Collect

To enable automated publishing and sync capabilities, our application requests access to:

  • YouTube Channel Data: Channel ID, channel title, handle, and subscriber/view statistics.
  • OAuth Credentials: Secure OAuth access and refresh tokens necessary to authenticate video uploads on your behalf.

3. How We Use the Information

The collected information is used strictly to:

  • Authenticate and upload authorized video content directly to your connected YouTube channels.
  • Display performance metrics and upload history on your private dashboard.

We do not sell, rent, or transfer your personal data or Google user credentials to any third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security

All authentication tokens are stored securely in encrypted or protected local configurations. Only the authorized administrator has access to the local database and tokens.

5. Revoking Access

You can revoke our application's access to your YouTube data at any time via the Google Security Settings page.
